Cycle 2 — Residential Sustainability & Energy Efficiency

Every client profile carries a different relationship to sustainability — from full LEED pursuit to simply keeping utility bills down. This cycle is about matching the strategy to the client.

11.2.111.2.211.2.3

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain how the building industry contributes to climate change and what LEED certification actually evaluates.

Apply

Apply a sustainability strategy to your row house unit consistent with your client's stated priorities.

Create

Create an envelope/orientation study defending your unit's sustainability approach at the first Design Critique.

Big Ideas & Essential Questions

11.2 — Residential Sustainability/Energy Efficiency
Big Idea(s)
  • The construction industry is responsible for 40% of the carbon emissions that influence climate change.
  • Architects must meet the needs of today without compromising the needs of the future.
Essential Question(s)
  • How does using sustainable concepts in the design of a building change the outcome of the project?
  • How do we best incorporate sustainable design concepts to create the most benefit to the environment while limiting economic impact?

Lesson Outline · 7 Days

  • Day 1Climate Change & the Building Industry
    Climate change fundamentals and the building industry's role. Revisit your client profile: what sustainability priority, if any, does your client have?
  • Day 2LEED Fundamentals
    LEED certification levels and credit categories. Introduce the LEED BD+C checklist.
  • Day 3Evaluating a Building Against LEED
    Evaluate a sample building against the LEED checklist in small groups.
  • Day 4Alternative Rating Systems
    WELL, SITES, PEER, GRESB — when is each used instead of or alongside LEED?
  • Day 5Studio Work Day
    Apply a sustainability strategy — orientation, envelope, or rating-system goal — to your unit. Revit: continue massing/envelope.
  • Day 6Design Critique #1 (Milestone)
    Present your unit so far — code compliance plus sustainability strategy — for verbal peer and teacher feedback.
  • Day 7Wrap-Up & Quiz Review
    Cycle Check-In Rubric review. Quiz 2 review session.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
