Cycle 3 — Electrical Systems & Row House Wrap-Up

The Row House project's final piece: a reflected ceiling/electrical plan that answers directly to the client's lighting needs, completing the unit's drawing set.

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain how power travels from breaker to fixture and what a reflected ceiling plan communicates.

Apply

Apply lighting specification and RCP conventions to your unit's specific client-driven lighting needs.

Create

Create a complete reflected ceiling/electrical plan, closing out the Row House drawing set.

Big Ideas & Essential Questions

11.3 — Electrical Systems
Big Idea(s)
  • Electrical and lighting systems are fundamental in the design and construction of a home.
  • Architects must provide adequate space in their designs to functionally power a home.
Essential Question(s)
  • How does electricity get into a home, and how is it distributed?
  • How do we competently communicate our design intentions to contractors and subcontractors?

Lesson Outline · 7 Days

  • Day 1Electrical Fundamentals
    Circuit path from breaker to fixture. AC/DC comparison. Revit: download light fixture families from a manufacturer's site for your client's stated lighting needs.
  • Day 2Lighting Specification & Math
    Calculating light output; matching fixture to task.
  • Day 3Reflected Ceiling Plan Conventions
    Lighting and switching symbols.
  • Day 4Studio Work Day
    Draft your unit's RCP/electrical plan, tying fixture choices back to your client profile.
  • Day 5Studio Work Day (cont.)
    Place light fixture families and callouts in Revit; add room labels and dimensions.
  • Day 6Peer Check-In
    Review a partner's RCP for missing symbols or mismatched fixture-to-task choices.
  • Day 7Row House Wrap-Up
    Cycle Check-In Rubric review. Quiz 3 review session. Row House project wraps — Final Project Rubric applied.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
