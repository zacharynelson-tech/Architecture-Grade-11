Big Ideas & Essential Questions

11.5 — HVAC
Big Idea(s)
  • HVAC systems are fundamental in the design and construction of a home.
  • Architects must provide adequate HVAC information to ensure the integrity of their designs.
Essential Question(s)
  • Why do architects need to know how the HVAC systems work in the homes they design?
  • How do we competently communicate our design intentions to contractors and subcontractors?

Lesson Outline · 7 Days

  • Day 1HVAC Fundamentals
    Heating/cooling cycle, duct space, thermostats, exhaust.
  • Day 2Fuel Types
    Oil, natural gas, electric — comparing cost, availability, and design implications.
  • Day 3HVAC Equipment Types
    Forced air, heat pump (air-to-air, split, geothermal), hydronic systems.
  • Day 4Studio Work Day
    Design mechanical space for the house and select a system consistent with the shared program.
  • Day 5Studio Work Day (cont.)
    Finalize equipment specification. Revit: model plans/sections showing mechanical space allocation.
  • Day 6Title Blocks & Plotting
    Apply title blocks, complete import/export and printing setup — closing out the House model as a complete, plottable drawing set.
  • Day 7House Wrap-Up
    Cycle Check-In Rubric review. House project wraps — Final Project Rubric applied. Quiz 5 review + DSA I study guide distributed.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
