Big Ideas & Essential Questions

11.4 — Plumbing Systems
Big Idea(s)
  • Plumbing systems are fundamental in the design and construction of a home.
  • Architects must provide adequate plumbing information to ensure the integrity of their designs.
Essential Question(s)
  • How do different types of plumbing fixtures/equipment change the design of our homes?
  • How do we competently communicate our design intentions to contractors and subcontractors?

Lesson Outline · 7 Days

  • Day 1House Kickoff & Plumbing Fundamentals
    Introduce the House project — a new single-family program. City vs. well/septic, stack diagrams. Revit: new project setup, continuing from Row House skills.
  • Day 2Plumbing Specifications
    Fixture types and specification sheets. Practice reading a manufacturer's plumbing spec sheet.
  • Day 3Kitchen Plumbing Layout
    Clearances and spatial allowances.
  • Day 4Bathroom Plumbing Layout
    Clearances and spatial allowances.
  • Day 5Studio Work Day
    Draft kitchen + bath plumbing layouts for the house. Revit: place plumbing fixture families into kitchen/bath.
  • Day 6Plumbing Schedule
    Create a plumbing schedule for the house. Revit: continue modeling millwork, appliances, and fixtures.
  • Day 7Wrap-Up & Quiz Review
    Cycle Check-In Rubric review. Quiz 4 review session.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
